About

BUILT FOR
COMPLEX
Markets.

Hadley Consulting is a senior advisory boutique delivering management consulting, public and corporate affairs, and strategic business development to investors, corporates, institutions and governments operating in high-growth, complex environments.

Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Hong Kong, with offices in London and Belgrade, Hadley combines deep strategic insight with on-the-ground presence across Southeast Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa, South and Southeast Asia, Latin America and selected frontier markets.

We operate at the intersection of capital, policy and execution — advising clients on matters where commercial, regulatory and political frameworks converge and where the quality of local knowledge and relationships determines outcomes.

Our Approach

HOW WE WORK

— 01
SENIOR-LED, ALWAYS

Every mandate is led by a principal with substantive operating experience in the relevant markets and sectors. We do not delegate to analysts or use junior staff to manage client relationships.

— 02
ON-THE-GROUND ACUMEN

Our principals have lived, worked and built networks in the markets they advise on. That depth of local knowledge — political, cultural and commercial — is not replicable from a distance.

— 03
INTEGRATED ACROSS DISCIPLINES

We combine management consulting, public affairs, strategic communications and transaction advisory. This integrated capability is particularly valuable in markets where regulatory, political and commercial dynamics are inseparable.

— 04
DISCREET BY DESIGN

Many of our most significant mandates involve sensitive commercial, political or reputational matters. We operate with the discretion that complex environments require and that experienced investors and institutions expect.

CASE STUDIES

SE Europe
MARKET ENTRY ADVISORY
Management Consulting

MARKET ENTRY FOR A EUROPEAN TECHNOLOGY INVESTOR

Advised a European technology investor on market entry into Southeast Europe, covering regulatory mapping, stakeholder identification and operating model options across multiple jurisdictions.

Engagement included assessment of the investment and regulatory environment, identification of potential local partners, and support for negotiations with public-sector counterparts. Delivered a phased market entry framework adopted by the client's board.

Outcome: Client established regional operations within 18 months, with Hadley providing ongoing government relations support.

West Africa
SOVEREIGN ADVISORY
Public & Corporate Affairs

INVESTOR RELATIONS & SOVEREIGN COMMUNICATIONS

Supported a West African government entity with investor relations, sovereign communications and FDI promotion targeting international institutional investors and development finance institutions.

Work included message development, preparation of investment materials and coordination of an international investor engagement programme spanning London and key European financial centres.

Outcome: Materially increased engagement with targeted DFIs and institutional investors; strengthened sovereign positioning in international capital markets.

Cross-Border
TRANSACTION SUPPORT
Transaction Advisory

CROSS-BORDER TRANSACTION & ARBITRATION SUPPORT

Provided advisory support in connection with a cross-border commercial dispute involving defence procurement between two sovereign states, including arbitration-related advisory, stakeholder engagement and diplomatic positioning.

Work required close coordination between legal counsel, government stakeholders and communications advisers across multiple jurisdictions and time zones.

Outcome: Supported successful resolution of a complex and sensitive cross-border matter through integrated advisory across legal, diplomatic and commercial dimensions.

SE Asia
INFRASTRUCTURE & ENVIRONMENT
Project Advisory

INFRASTRUCTURE & ENVIRONMENTAL PROGRAMME ADVISORY

Advised on major infrastructure and environmental initiatives in Southeast Asia, supporting both public and private sector stakeholders in navigating regulatory approvals, stakeholder engagement and public communications.

REGIONAL INVESTMENT PROMOTION & BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT
